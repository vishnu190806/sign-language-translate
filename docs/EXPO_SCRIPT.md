# Expo Script (M13, ~60 seconds)

**0–10 s — Hook.** "This laptop watches Indian Sign Language and speaks English — fully offline, no cloud." Open `run_live_demo.bat`, camera live.

**10–30 s — Words.** Sign DOG, PEACE, BICYCLE slowly, one second each. Narrate: "583-word vocabulary, three specialist models voting — hands, pose, 168 measurements per frame, 50 frames a second."

**30–45 s — Sentence.** Sign YOU → GOOD, pause. System speaks: "you are good." Narrate: "Glosses become grammar — rules plus a local language model — then a neural voice."

**45–55 s — Proof.** Press `D`. "Every number live: per-model confidence, the stability gate deciding when a sign counts, millisecond latency. Nothing hidden."

**55–60 s — Close.** "No internet. No data leaves this laptop. ISL in, English out." `Q` to quit.

**If anything misfires:** stay on the scripted list (100%-recall classes), slow down, and use the fallbacks in `docs/demo_video_guide.md`. Never improvise vocabulary on stage.
